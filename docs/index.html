The problem

Every Hermes release changes how your agent thinks: the prompt, the tools, the hooks. You usually find out from a session that went wrong. Ariadne lets you find out first, offline, for free.

How it works
01 / RECORD

Keep the thread.

Turn recording on and use Hermes as usual. Every model call, tool call and turn is saved to a cassette on your machine, with secrets scrubbed. Nothing leaves your computer.

02 / REPLAY

Follow it back.

Ariadne answers every model call and tool call from the cassette, while the rest of Hermes runs for real. No model, no network, no tokens. If anything Hermes sends is different, you see the exact line.

03 / COMPARE

Two installs, one thread.

Replay one cassette on two Hermes installs side by side with compare. What differs between the machines cancels out; what Hermes changed does not.

04 / BISECT

Name the commit.

bisect replays across a Hermes checkout and names the first commit that changed your session. 358 commits, 10 replays, about a minute.

05 / FORK

Take another path.

Replay up to any turn, then hand the session to a live model from exactly that state. Tools stay frozen unless you allow them. Export the result as training data.

Finding 001

A truncated reply skipped post_api_request.

Hermes promises plugins that every pre_api_request ends with a post_api_request or an error. When a model reply was cut off at the token limit, it did neither. Ariadne's contract check caught it on a replay; the fix is a few lines with a test that fails before it.

Issue #150324 ↗ Fix #150333 ↗

✓ post_llm_call fires at most once per turn
